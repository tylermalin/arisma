Security & attestation.

Trust rooted in hardware, verifiable at every layer.

company / security

Every mandate is signed by a key held in hardware: a confidential VM for cloud agents, a TPM for physical machines. Physical readings are signed by a known gateway, and high-stakes physical actions wait for a person before anything is released.

The threat model is Chapter 6 of the whitepaper. The economic bounds are Chapter 5. For audit coordination or a security disclosure, reach the team directly.

Guarantees.

Hardware attestation

A confidential VM's attestation report, or an X1 gateway's TPM quote, is checked before a mandate is issued.

Policy checks

The gate checks signed readings against the policy before release. Policy proofs that hide the inputs are on the roadmap.

Signed telemetry

Audits

Responsible disclosure

If you believe you have found a vulnerability in the protocol, an enclave image, or a published package, report it privately before any public disclosure. We acknowledge reports quickly and coordinate a fix and release window with you.

  • 01Report privately to security@arisma.network, with steps to reproduce.
  • 02Acknowledge within one business day, with a tracking reference.
  • 03Coordinate a fix, an enclave re-attestation if needed, and a disclosure date.
