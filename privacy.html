Privacy.

What this site collects, why, and who handles it.

legal / privacy

Last updated November 3, 2026

What we collect

This site collects only what you type into its forms: your name, work email, organization, intended use, and any message. We do not set advertising or analytics cookies.

Why we collect it

We use it to answer your request: reviewing access applications, replying to messages, and sending the status alerts you sign up for. We do not sell it or share it for marketing.

Who handles it

The site is hosted on Vercel. Form submissions are delivered to our team by email through Resend. Both process the data only to provide those services.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete what you sent us, or to stop status alerts, contact the team and we will act on it.
