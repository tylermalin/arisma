The Arisma trust & settlement stack.

Three unified layers bridging cryptographic intent with physical execution.

platform / architecture

Traditional payment rails are built for human speed and credit risk. Autonomous agents and physical machinery require cryptographic determinism, subsecond finality, and provable safety bounds. Arisma unifies this into three modular layers.

Three layers.

Verifiable agent identity & delegation

Session keys scoped by ERC-7702 and bound directly to a hardware enclave (AMD SEV-SNP / Intel TDX). Agents carry provable authorization without exposing master private keys.
